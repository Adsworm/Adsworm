# Retention rules (short form)

Full reasoning and sources: `docs/playbook/EDITING-PSYCHOLOGY.md`, `docs/playbook/REEL-PLAYBOOK.md`. Evidence labels there: study / weak / folklore / inference. Treat numbers as starting values.

## What Instagram says it ranks on
Watch time, likes, sends (sends weigh more with non-followers). Skip rate = left within 3 s. Originality counts; engagement bait and mostly-text reels are down-ranked; AI content must be labelled. Our KPIs: skip rate, retention slope, sends per reach.

## Numbers to hit
| Check | Target |
|---|---|
| First spoken word | by frame 6 |
| Frame 0 | finished board, both faces in frame, hook claim readable by frame 15 |
| On-screen changes | ≥ 3 in the first 3 s; then one every 2–4 s; never > 2 per second |
| Speaker hold | ≤ 6 s without a swap or a listener reaction |
| Reactions | ≥ 4 per reel, within 6 frames of the trigger word |
| Caption chunk | ≤ 4 words, ≤ 20 characters/s, ≥ 0.5 s |
| Board state | ≤ 7 readable words, one hero number, labels ≤ 3 words, read-me text ≥ 40 px |
| Payoff | at 55–70% of the runtime, after a 0.3–0.4 s breath |
| CTA keyword | on screen ≥ 2 s, said once |
| Sound | pad ≥ 15 LU under voice; effects ≥ 6 dB under voice; ≤ 25 effects per 50 s; master −14 LUFS, −1.5 dBTP |
| Length | 40–50 s, 95–115 words |

## What counts as a change
New element, state flip, sticker, speaker swap, reaction, punch-in, section whip. Not: karaoke highlight, mouth flap, background drift.

## Psychology in one page
- **Curiosity needs a visible gap.** Hook uncertain, payoff certain; keep the open question on screen (loop chip) until it is answered.
- **Each cut costs attention.** Fast pace reduced intent to finish in experiments: change with purpose, on the word, not on a metronome.
- **Say and show the same thing at the same moment.** Elements land on their word; no sentences on the board while someone talks; 2–3-word labels help.
- **Voice changes never get old; repeated effects do.** Short turns, and vary effect pitch.
- **Sharing follows usefulness, surprise, awe, anxiety, anger; sadness kills it.** Hook on the loss, CTA on the gain.
- **The student's mistake must be visible with the sound off**: her face, her cursor heading for the wrong button, the red stamp.
- **Colour means something**: blue = teacher and truth, pink = student and the mistake, red/green only for bad/good, gold for numbers and stickers.

## CTA rules (Instagram, June 2024 clarification)
A keyword that delivers a real thing by DM, said once, is fine. Never "comment YES", "tag 3 friends", "like for part 2". The share prompt names a person. A save prompt needs a save-worthy frame held ≥ 1.5 s.
