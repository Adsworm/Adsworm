# Leevora Aesthetic Clinic: GBP SEO Keyword Strategy (v1)

_Research date: 10 Nov 2026. Status: framework locked, waiting on city/locality + service menu to finalise._

## 1. Brand research findings
- **Online footprint for "Leevora": none.** No GBP, website, Instagram, Justdial or Practo listing is indexed yet.
  - Upside: a unique name means branded search is easy to own.
  - Downside: no existing data to build on.
- **Look-alike names already exist** (Levo Aesthetics Gurgaon, Leora Aesthetic Mumbai, Lèvere Clinics Delhi). Use the exact spelling "Leevora Aesthetic Clinic" everywhere so Google doesn't merge or confuse entities.

## 3. The core truth: one "magic word" won't rank you
| Lever | Weight | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Primary GBP category | #1 local pack factor | Getting this wrong is the #1 negative factor |
| Proximity | ~55% of ranking variance | Can't change it, but you win your 2–3 km radius |
| Reviews (velocity + text) | ~20% | A steady weekly flow; reviews that mention the treatment and the area |
| GBP posts | **Not a direct ranking factor** | They help through engagement and "post justifications" (Google shows your post snippet under the listing when it matches the query) |
| Keyword stuffing in posts | **New negative factor (Whitespark 2026)** | 1 primary + 1 secondary keyword per post, max |

So the caption keywords exist to **trigger justifications and conversions**. The ranking itself comes from category, services, reviews and the name.

## 4. Keyword architecture

### Master anchor (in every post, naturally)
**"Skin & Aesthetic Clinic in [Locality], [City]"**
- Indians search "skin clinic" and "dermatologist" far more than "aesthetic clinic". Anchoring on "skin" captures the volume, and "aesthetic" keeps the premium positioning.
- Never write "near me" in the copy. Google resolves "near me" by proximity, and in the copy it just reads as stuffing. Use the real locality name instead.

### Brand phrase (every post)
**"Leevora Aesthetic Clinic"**

### Treatment clusters (one per post; rotate)
| Cluster | Primary keyword | Secondary variants |
|---|---|---|
| Hair-free | laser hair removal in [Locality] | full body laser hair removal, painless laser hair removal |
| Glow | HydraFacial in [Locality] | medi-facial, glass skin facial, skin boosters |
| Pigmentation | pigmentation treatment in [City] | melasma treatment, dark spots, tan removal, chemical peel |
| Acne | acne scar treatment in [City] | acne treatment, open pores, MNRF / laser resurfacing |
| Anti-ageing | anti-ageing treatment in [City] | Botox, fillers, fine lines, under-eye treatment |
| Hair | hair fall treatment in [City] | PRP / GFC hair treatment, hair regrowth |
| Bridal (Nov–Feb peak, **live now**) | bridal skin package in [City] | pre-wedding glow, groom skincare |

### Premium modifiers (pick one; never "cheap/discount")
advanced · doctor-led · US-FDA-approved laser (only if true) · personalised · luxury

## 5. Caption formula (Google Posts)
1. **First line** (shown in the snippet): treatment + locality + outcome. Example: "Laser hair removal in [Locality]: smooth skin in fewer sessions."
2. **Body** (80–150 words): problem → doctor-led solution → why Leevora → session/time detail.
3. Mention the brand once, the primary keyword twice at most, and one secondary keyword.
4. CTA button: **Book** or **Call**. Link with UTM: `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=gbp&utm_campaign=post`.
5. Use a real clinic photo or video. No stock images, no text-heavy creatives.
6. Cadence: 2–3 posts a week, rotating clusters.

## 6. Off-post levers (bigger impact than captions)
- **Name:** exactly "Leevora Aesthetic Clinic". Adding keywords to the name gets you suspended.
- **Primary category:** "Dermatologist" only if an MD dermatologist practises there. Otherwise "Skin care clinic".
- **Secondary categories** (up to 4): Laser hair removal service, Medical spa, Facial spa, Hair removal service. Check the exact options in the GBP dropdown.
- **Services section:** one entry per treatment, each with a 300-character keyword-rich description.
- **Reviews:** ask every patient to "mention the treatment you had". No incentives and no gating.
- **Citations (India):** Practo, Justdial, Bing Places (feeds ChatGPT and Copilot), Apple Business Connect. Keep NAP identical everywhere.
- **Photos:** 5+ a week for the first 8 weeks (exterior, reception, doctor, machines, team).

## 7. Compliance flags
- Never claim "guaranteed", "permanent", "100%" or "instant" results, or name competitors (Google healthcare policy, NMC ethics, DMR Act).
- Before/after photos need patient consent. Keep them off GBP early on, because they trigger removals.
- Don't promote IV glutathione "skin whitening" (regulatory and policy risk).
