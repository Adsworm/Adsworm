## How to read this data
- **Google autocomplete only suggests queries that real people search often enough.**
  - A suggestion appearing = proven demand.
  - **No suggestion = very low demand.**
- **Autocomplete shows that demand exists, not how much.**
  - For exact monthly volumes, open Keyword Planner in your own Google Ads account (free) and paste in the Tier A/B lists below.
  - Treat those numbers as the final confirmation.

## 2. Rules: what goes in every line (and what doesn't)
**Don't repeat the keyword in every line.** Keyword stuffing in posts is a ranking *negative* (Whitespark 2026), and Google reads meaning, not repetition.

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Line 1 | Primary keyword + area. This is what shows in the snippet |
| Body | 1 secondary keyword + the brand once + natural related words (sessions, Indian skin, hair fall, scalp…). Google understands related words semantically |
| Location | Address area (Subhash Nagar) once, Rajouri Garden/West Delhi once |
| Total | Primary keyword max 2×, in a 100–180 word post |
