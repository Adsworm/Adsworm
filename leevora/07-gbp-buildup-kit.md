# Notes before you publish

**Check these against what you actually offer, and delete what you don't:**
- Hair colour: "global colour, highlights and root touch-ups" (Post 5, service list)
- Laser areas: "face, underarms, arms, legs or back" (Post 2); "chest, back and shoulders" (Post 12)
- Fillers: "lips, under-eyes, cheeks" (Post 11)
- Exosomes are written for hair fall only (Post 10). If you also offer them for skin, say so and a skin version can be added.
- "Cryotherapy" in your brief was taken to mean fat freezing (cryolipolysis).
- If your extensions are permanent, add the word "permanent" to Post 9: "permanent hair extensions in rajouri garden" is a real search.

**Botox:** the posts say "anti-wrinkle injections", the same as your creative. Botox is a prescription drug, and Google can reject posts that promote one. "Botox treatment in west delhi" is a real search, so if the doctor is comfortable with it, the place to use the word is the Services list name: "Anti-wrinkle injections (Botox)".

**Website gap:** the site has no page yet for hair colour, hair extensions, exosomes, PDRN, anti-wrinkle injections or fillers. Google trusts a service more when the website has a page for it.
