# 02 — Account data: was October a "bleeding month"?

## Method
- Meta Ads account-level numbers, pulled read-only on 12 Nov 2026. 55 accounts listed, 43 readable, **18 kept** (spend ≥ ₹5,000 in both September and October, and spend > 0 on 9–11 Nov).
- Periods: A = 1–30 Sep, B = 1–31 Oct, C = 1–8 Nov (Diwali week), D = 9–11 Nov.
- CPM = spend ÷ impressions × 1,000. CPC = spend ÷ link clicks. CTR = link clicks ÷ impressions.
- Result = messaging conversations started, the largest action in September for all 18 accounts. Cost per result (CPR) is skipped where a period has fewer than 10 results.
- Medians are across accounts. Pooled = total spend ÷ total volume.

No account tracking purchases qualified, so there is no lead/messaging vs purchase split.

## Verdict
- **October (B vs A): PARTLY SUPPORTED.** Results cost more: cost per conversation rose in 15 of 17 accounts, median +16%, pooled +12%. But ad prices did not rise: CPM and cost per click were slightly lower and CTR was flat.
- **Post-Diwali (D vs B): NOT SUPPORTED** as "better than October". Cost per result is a median 6.5% higher, worse in 8 of 16 accounts, and cost per click is up 17.5%. What the data does show is a drop from the Diwali-week peak, back to about October's level and still about 12% above September.

## Caveats
- D is three days (Mon–Wed) with 11–119 results per account. It could reverse within a week.
- 18 of 55 accounts. Twelve could not be read through the tool; the rest had little or no spend, or none after Diwali.
- A conversation started is not a booking. Lead quality is not measured.
- Some movement is the account, not the market: one account roughly tripled spend, and account 12's +766% reflects a change in what its campaigns were buying.
- One baseline month and no year-on-year comparison. Timing next to Diwali is not proof of cause.

## Safe on-screen lines
1. "In 15 of 17 Adsworm ad accounts, cost per conversation rose from September to October (median +16%)."
2. "Median CPM across 18 accounts was 5% lower in October than in September."
3. "Diwali week was the peak: blended cost per conversation ₹210, vs ₹165 in October, ₹147 in September."
4. "In the 3 days after Diwali, cost per conversation fell from Diwali week in 13 of 16 accounts."

Do not say: "CPMs went up in October", "costs are back to normal", or "performance is better than October".
