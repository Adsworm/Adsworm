## Caption

Ads pe paise laga rahe ho, phir bhi wahi thode log dekh rahe hain? 😩

Ek baar apne ads saath mein rakh ke dekho. Agar paancho ek jaise dikhte hain (wahi photo, wahi baat, bas rang alag), toh Instagram ki machine unhe ek hi ad samajh sakti hai. Phir aap kam logon tak pahunchte ho.

Is machine ko log aajkal "Andromeda 2.0" keh rahe hain.

Karna kya hai? Har ad alag banao:
1️⃣ Ek mein apna kaam ya saamaan dikhao
2️⃣ Doosre mein customer ki tareef
3️⃣ Teesre mein offer

Post boost karte ho? Wo bhi ad hi hai, wahan bhi yahi rule.
