# Facts allowed in the Andromeda reel (from 01-research.md; rows in brackets)

You may say these, in plain words, no stronger than written. Nothing else is a fact.

1. Andromeda is the part of Meta's ads system that makes the shortlist: for each person it narrows "tens of millions of ads to the few thousand worth ranking". An ad that is not on the shortlist is not shown to that person. (B1, B2; Meta for Business + Meta Engineering, 28 Oct 2026)
2. Meta rebuilt it to be much faster: it now scores "over one billion ads per second", about 100 times the 2024 version. That is the machine's speed, not a promise about your results. (B3, P5)
3. Because it looks at more ads, "more of your ads, creative variations and products are considered for each person". (W2)
4. "Andromeda 2.0" is a nickname used by creators and tool companies. Meta's own post calls it "the next generation of Meta Andromeda"; the text "2.0" is not in it. (N1–N3)
5. Meta says: "There's no setting to switch on, no new campaign type to adopt, no migration to plan." It reaches every account automatically. (W1)
6. Meta says: when ads look too similar, its system "may not recognize it as different and could deliver it to the same sets of audiences", which limits reach and leads to a "Creative limited" or "Creative fatigue" status. (W3)
7. Meta's advice: "supply more creative variety". Meta also says its system "rewards a diverse mix more than repeated variations of similar assets". (W4, W7)
8. We found no Meta page that says how many ads you must run. (M2) Any number is Adsworm's own advice and must be called that.
9. Your targeting rules (for example your city) are still obeyed. (W5)

Not allowed: "rebuild your account", "one ad set only", "30 ads", "targeting is dead", any claim that Andromeda raised or lowered someone's results, any date on which it "launched".
Adsworm's own advice (say "humari salaah" if a number is used): make ads that differ in what they show (the product in use, a customer's review, the offer, the owner on camera).
