## Scores

- Understanding: 20+15+15+10+15+10+5+0 = **90**
- Enjoyment: 20+7.5+7.5+5+15+10+5+0 = **70**
- Watching after 5 s: **95** (80 if viewer 5 goes)

## Lines that lost people, worst first

1. L12–13, boost. Viewer 7 (10) cannot say what it meant. Viewer 5 (15) follows only if she knows the button. Viewers 3–4 (25) hear "not for me". Confused: 10; at risk: 25.
2. L14, "Comment mein ANDROMEDA likho". Viewer 7 (10) cannot do it; 1 and 5 (35) must copy a foreign word in 5 s. "Ready namoone" hides what arrives.
3. L6, "Andromeda 2.0". Everyone hears "a name", so confused: 0. But 1, 5, 7 (45) cannot repeat it, and L14 depends on it.
4. L1, "Paanch ads chala rahi hoon". Describes only viewers 3–4 (25). Loses 8 (5), endangers 5 (15).
5. L7 repeats L4; 18–32 s is Aman alone. Viewers 3–4 (25) drift.
6. L15, "Save kar liya!". Breaks the face-to-face scene; sounds like an advert.

## Terms never explained

"2.0"; "boost" (shown, called an ad, never defined); "ads chalana" (where, how); "ready namoone". "Machine" waits two lines; Riya's question covers it.

## Pace and load

Not too fast. One idea, said twice over 20 s; then boost, comment and save crowd the last 14 s.

## Picture against words

- L2, L4: same photo, then "=" one card. Carry the idea better than the words.
- L6: pile, box, name in two scripts, captions, in 7 s. Helps, but crowded.
- L8, L11: crowd with two lit, then three cards reaching three different people. This explains "kam log"; the words only assert it.
- L13: fights. Boost pressed, post flies to more phones: "boost works". The words mean "boost follows the same rule".

## Voice direction

- L4: stress only on "ek – hi – ad" lets "machine" slip past, leaving Riya's "Machine?" nothing to catch. Lean lightly on "machine".
- L6: needs a beat between sentences, or the name lands on the explanation.
- L7: "plain, steady" on a repeated line is the flattest moment. Stress "ek jaise … ek hi".
- L12: "brighter" and quick throws away "aise hi". Stress "boost bhi", pause before "aise hi".
- L13: "warm, sure" over a success picture sounds like approval of boosting. Make it matter-of-fact; stress "bhi" and "ad hi".

## VERDICT: PASS

Understanding 90 (bar 85). Viewers 1, 2, 5 YES and 7 PARTLY, each with the right action. Worst line confuses 10 (limit 25). 95 watching after 5 s (bar 70). Thin spots: the boost exchange is one viewer type from the limit; enjoyment is only 70.
