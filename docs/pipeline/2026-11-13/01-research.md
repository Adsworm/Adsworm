## D. Candidate reels (R relatability · U useful today · N novelty · E explainable in 50 s · L lead potential)

### D1. "Andromeda 2.0 is not a Meta name" (news + myth, Andromeda) · R4 U3 N5 E5 L4 = **21**
- **Why now:** the label took off after 28 Oct (C1, C2) and there are few Hinglish explainers yet (C7). **Riya:** "Andromeda 2.0 aa gaya! Ab poora account dobara banana padega?"
- **Truth:** it is a nickname for a system Meta upgraded in the background, and Meta says there is nothing to switch on (N1, W1). **Analogy:** the colony's transformer was changed, not your house wiring. Supply got better for everyone; what you plug in is still up to you.
- **Facts:** Meta's post never says "2.0" (VERIFIED, N1) · "no setting to switch on, no new campaign type to adopt, no migration to plan" (VERIFIED, W1) · "a 2.4% increase in conversions on Facebook" since 2025 (VERIFIED, P2, Meta's own figure).
- **Save card:** "Andromeda 2.0: 3 true, 3 not true." **CTA:** **ANDROMEDA** → DM: a one-page PDF, "What Meta actually said on 28 Oct": 5 quotes with links, plus 3 questions to ask before you rebuild anything.
- **Risk:** "nothing to switch on" is not "nothing changed"; say nickname, not fake; the 2.4% is self-reported.

### D2. "Your targeting moved into your ad" (the reference reel's topic) · R5 U5 N3 E4 L5 = **22**
- **Why now:** the owner's reference; "the new way to advertise" framing is live (C5). **Riya:** "Paanch ad sets, paanch audiences, har ek mein wahi teen ads. Targeting hi toh mera kaam hai!"
- **Truth:** Meta says the focus has shifted from narrow targeting to creative variety, and look-alike ads get shown to the same people (W6, W3). **Analogy:** the mannequin outside your shop. A sherwani on it brings grooms; a school uniform brings parents. Three mannequins in the same kurta bring the same crowd.
- **Facts:** "from niche targeting to creative diversification as the best lever" (VERIFIED, W6) · "When creative is too similar… could deliver it to the same sets of audiences" (VERIFIED, W3) · your location and age rules are still enforced (VERIFIED, W5).
- **Save card:** "Old way → new way", 3 rows: who finds the buyer · what you make · what you still set. **CTA:** **TARGET** → DM: an "ad angle sheet" (5 reasons people buy × 3 formats) to plan 5 clearly different ads, with the 5-point "is it really different?" check.
- **Risk:** do not repeat "30 ads", "one ad set" or "3 ads = 3 kinds of people" (Part A). Lead with the ad, not with fewer ad sets (made on 12 Nov).

### D3. "20 ads that look the same are one ad" (Andromeda, creative similarity) · R4 U5 N4 E5 L4 = **22**
- **Why now:** Meta's 19 Oct post (W7) and the new Creative diversity column (W8). **Riya:** "Maine 20 ads banaye! Har ek mein background ka colour alag hai."
- **Truth:** if ads look alike, Meta may not see them as different and may show them to the same people (W3). **Analogy:** a mithai counter with 20 trays, all kaju katli. The customer who wanted rasgulla walks on.
- **Facts:** the W3 quote (VERIFIED) · "rewards a diverse mix more than repeated variations of similar assets" (VERIFIED, W7) · a High/Medium/Low "Creative diversity" column compares your images and video thumbnails (PARTLY, W8).
- **Save card:** "Same ad or different ad? 5 checks": first 3 seconds · person or place · format · reason to buy · who it is for (Adsworm's list, not Meta's). **CTA:** **SAME** → DM: the 5-check card plus where to look in Ads Manager (the Delivery column; Columns → Customize columns → Creative diversity).
- **Risk:** "20 = 1" is a figure of speech, Meta gives no threshold; the column path is secondhand and may not be in every account.

### D4. "You don't need 30 ads" (myth, small budgets) · R5 U4 N4 E4 L4 = **21**
- **Why now:** the reference reel says "up to 30", and small-budget videos are the biggest (C3). **Riya:** "Reel mein bola 30 alag ads chahiye. Main akeli hoon, 30 kahan se laun?"
- **Truth:** we found no Meta page that sets a number; Meta asks for different ads, not more ads (W6, W9). **Analogy:** a playing eleven. You need a batter, a bowler and a keeper, not 30 openers, and only eleven get to play.
- **Facts:** Meta's creative-diversification post gives no number (VERIFIED, W6) · Meta's guidance is "deliberately outcome-based (not model-specific)" (PARTLY, W9) · "too many ads can result in worse performance" (PARTLY, W10).
- **Save card:** "My playing XI of ads": problem · proof · offer · founder · demo · comparison. **CTA:** **KITNE** → DM: the playing-XI sheet with one example line per role, and our start point of 4–6 clearly different ads (marked as Adsworm's advice).
- **Risk:** any number we give is ours, not Meta's; big spenders do run far more (W7's case mentions "hundreds of active ads per account").

### D5. "Targeting is not dead. Your city still counts." (myth, local businesses) · R4 U4 N3 E4 L4 = **19**
- **Why now:** "Meta Ads Manual Targeting is Dead" titles are circulating (youtu.be/YiZ1AGVylaw, 172 views, 3 d). **Riya:** "Targeting toh dead hai na? Maine city aur age bhi hata di. Full broad!"
