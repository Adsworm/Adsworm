## What gives these two a chance to spread (and what does not)

- **The hook keeps the scare word and pays it off with relief.** YouTube titles with "changed forever" / "2.0 is here" pulled 23–80K views in two weeks; calm myth-busting titles pulled hundreds. So Riya panics ("Andromeda 2.0 aa gaya!") and Aman deflates it with a receipt.

- **Each reel ends on a card worth saving** (sach/afwah; "alag ad ka test"), a share line that names a friend, and one comment keyword with a real DM.
- **The reference reel proves nothing yet:** 900 followers, 16 likes four hours after posting. We took its diagram idea and dropped its three unsourced claims.
- Nobody can promise a reel goes viral. After 48 hours note: skip rate in the first 3 s, the retention graph, saves and sends per reach, keyword comments.
