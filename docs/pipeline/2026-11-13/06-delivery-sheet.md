Rules used:
- One mood tag at the start of a line; dots ("…") where the speaker stops for a beat; an exclamation mark where the energy rises.
- The held breath before line 10 ("Har ad alag banao!") is added in the edit (riser, a third of a second of silence, a hit), so that line lands hardest.
- English words go to the voice in Devanagari ("ads" → "ऐड्स") so the voice does not change its pronunciation mid-sentence. Captions stay in Roman Hinglish.
- Pace: the voice is sped up by 6% only. Pauses longer than 0.3 s are trimmed, laughs and gasps are kept.
