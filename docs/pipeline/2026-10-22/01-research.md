## 1. "Why your ads suddenly feel expensive this October"
**Why now:** The festive/Q4 auction is heating up. Dhanteras falls on 6 Nov, Diwali on 8 Nov and Black Friday on 27 Nov. It speaks directly to "October is down".
**Facts:**
- Meta's official Q2 2026 results: ad impressions **+14%** YoY and average price per ad **+12%** YoY. Source: https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Results/ (late Jul 2026; the figures also appear in the 10-Q at https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001326801/000162828026050705/meta-20260630.htm).
- Lebesgue looked at $22.6M of Meta spend from Sep to Nov 2025. The median brand's CPM ran **+59%** vs September in Black Friday week and **+84%** on BF day. Cost per purchase still **fell 20%** because conversion rate **rose 48%**. In 2024 the CPM climb began in **mid-October**. Source: https://lebesgue.io/?p=15192 (16 Oct 2026). This is a vendor dataset, mostly US/Shopify brands.
- Razorpay says Google CPCs "can climb 25–40%" in the Diwali window. It cites no source, so treat this as **UNVERIFIED**. https://rizevault.razorpay.com/p/ad-spend-and-cac-survival-guide-for-festive-season (8 Sep 2026)

**Pain:** "I'm spending the same and getting fewer leads/sales."
**Angle/CTA:** It isn't you, the auction got pricier. Brands that win fix conversion rate instead of cutting spend. "Comment **OCTOBER** and we'll audit where your money is leaking before Diwali."
**Scores:** T5 S4 L5 E5 = **19**

## 2. "Meta will change your running ads on 28 Oct unless you opt out"
**Why now:** It's a hard deadline 6 days away and it happens by default.
**Facts:**
- A Meta advertiser notice says: "Starting October 28, 2026, we'll add product browsing to your existing ads." A tap may open an in-app product page before your site. Source: https://ppc.land/meta-will-turn-on-product-browsing-in-existing-ads-on-october-28/ (15 Oct 2026). Also https://www.relevantaudience.com/meta/meta-product-browsing-existing-ads-october-28/
- Meta claims **+3%** conversions on average. That comes from a 6–21 Aug 2026 test across about **117,700** advertisers. The notice doesn't define the conversion metric.
- You can opt out with the notice button before 28 Oct, or per ad with the Advantage+ creative/destination toggle.
- **Caveat:** PPC Land found **no Meta Help Center or newsroom page**, so the only source is the notice as screenshotted by a media buyer. Present it as "Meta told advertisers", not as an official announcement.

**Pain:** "Meta changes my ads without asking. Will it hurt my tracking or send buyers somewhere else?"
**Angle/CTA:** "Check this toggle before Tuesday. If you're not sure whether it helps you, DM **BROWSE** and we'll check your account." Best fit is e-commerce/D2C.
**Scores:** T5 S2 L4 E4 = **15**

## 3. "Amazon & Flipkart did ₹24,200 crore in 2 days, so why is your shop quiet?"
**Why now:** Festive sale results came out this month. Owners see "record sales" headlines while their own numbers are flat.
**Facts:**
- Redseer reports more than **$2.5B (~₹24,200 cr)** in GMV across Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho and Ajio in the first two days of the festive sales. Source: https://telanganatoday.com/festive-sales-e-commerce-platforms-clock-rs-24000-crore-in-two-days (11 Oct 2026).
- Redseer projects the festive season to grow about **25%**, the strongest in about 5 years, with GMV of $15–16B. Quick commerce is expected to grow **110–120%** YoY against **16–18%** for the rest of e-commerce. Source: https://www.theweek.in/news/biz-tech/2026/09/18/india-festive-online-retail-growth.html (18 Sep 2026); https://bwretailworld.com/sector/e-commerce-marketplaces/india-online-retail-set-to-cross-90-bn-in-2026
- Counterpoint says the season will be "healthy in value terms, but more measured in volume terms". I saw this only in a search snippet of newkerala.com (26 Sep 2026) because the page returned 403, so it is **UNVERIFIED as opened**.

**Pain:** "Demand exists but it's going to the big platforms."
**Angle/CTA:** Demand didn't disappear, it moved. The fix is to show up where buyers are scrolling during the 6–8 Nov window. "DM **DIWALI** for a 15-min festive plan."
**Scores:** T4 S3 L4 E5 = **16**

## 4. "Google is now adding discounts to your ads by itself"
**Facts:**
- From **12 Oct 2026**, Google auto-extracts promotions from your website into Search and PMax campaigns that have linked location assets. It is opt-out under account-level automated assets. Source: https://seroundtable.com/google-ads-automated-promotions-october-12-42223.html (5 Oct 2026)
- Image extraction will be on by default for new PMax campaigns from **4 Dec 2026**. Source: https://ppc.land/google-turns-on-pmax-image-extraction-by-default-from-december-4/ (seen in results; date of the article not opened)

**Pain:** Expired or wrong offers could show up in your ads, which matters most for local businesses and clinics.
**CTA:** "Comment **GOOGLE** and we'll check what Google auto-turned on in your account."
**Scores:** T4 S2 L3 E4 = **13**

## 6. "Meta's AI can now plan and run your ads. Should you let it?" (AI-news hook)
**Facts (official Meta, 6 Oct 2026, https://www.facebook.com/business/news/advertising-week-new-york-2026):**
- Advertisers who follow the Meta AI business assistant's recommendations typically see "**5% more conversions at the same cost per conversion**."
- Agentic campaign creation, budget changes and scheduled alerts are **in testing**. The assistant now remembers past quarters, seasonality and goals.
- Plain-language "describe your ideal customer" audience discovery comes to Detailed Targeting "by the end of the year".
- The Meta ads MCP server now has 10 integration partners. Every action needs advertiser approval before it goes live.

**Pain:** "Do I still need an agency if AI runs ads?"
**Angle/CTA:** AI is only as good as your tracking, offer and creative inputs. "Comment **AI** to see if your account is AI-ready." (Avoid repeating the image-to-video item.)
**Scores:** T4 S3 L3 E4 = **14**

**Pain:** "Sales are flat even though the ads get lots of clicks." This is a common, evergreen worry. The search demand behind "facebook ads not working"-type queries is **UNVERIFIED** because no volume data was available.
