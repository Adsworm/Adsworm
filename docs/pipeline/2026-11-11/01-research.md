**Why now:** Only AI-news reels have reached 48–55K. The newest item is 21 Oct, just before the window.

## 4. "Your leads aren't bad. You're late." (relatable) · R5 U5 N2 E5 L5 = **22**
**Why evergreen:** Clinics, coaches and service owners all say "leads don't pick up". The fix is their own behaviour, not an "ads tip".
- HBR, Mar 2011, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads": "most companies are not responding nearly fast enough." https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads (intro only; paywall)
- Figures via secondary: 2,241 US companies audited; 37% replied within an hour; 23% never; average 42 hours; within the hour "nearly seven times as likely" to qualify the lead. https://www.smartcompany.com.au/marketing/20110704-are-you-letting-online-leads-go-cold/ (1 Jul 2011). Old US data: card only, not a spoken India fact.
- Meta's Business AI in the WhatsApp Business app (India) answers 24/7. https://about.fb.com/news/2026/05/introducing-business-ai-on-whatsapp-for-small-businesses-in-india/ (May 2026; snippet)

**Riya:** "These Instagram leads are useless. I called next morning, nobody picked up!"
**Analogy:** A customer walks into your shop and you say hello three hours later.
**CTA:** **REPLY** → first-reply WhatsApp script + 5-minute follow-up checklist.
