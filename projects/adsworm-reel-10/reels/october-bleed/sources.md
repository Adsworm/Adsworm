## What the reel does not claim

- **It does not say ad prices rose in our accounts.** They did not: median CPM was about 5% lower in October than in September. Results cost more because fewer people enquired per click. "Har brand ek saath bid karta hai" is the general festive pattern, and the only number shown for it is Criteo's, labelled 2025.
- **It does not say costs are back to normal or better than October.** The three days after Diwali are back to about October's level. Three days can reverse.
- **"Customer sale mein busy tha" is our reading**, not a measured cause. It fits the published picture (Redseer via Inc42, 23 Oct 2026: about 100 million shoppers on the big online sales, 8–18 Oct; Counterpoint: "Platforms have seen strong traffic, but conversions have been weak").
- "Enquiry" on the chart is a message conversation started. It is not a booking or an order.
- The bidders (₹50, ₹120, ₹200), the shop, the daily budget stickers and the roti with ten plates are illustrations.

No client or account names appear in the reel or in these files.
