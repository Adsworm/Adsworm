## What the reel does not claim

- **It does not say ad prices rose in our accounts.** They did not: median CPM was about 5% lower in October than in September. Enquiries cost more because fewer people enquired per click. "Har brand ek saath bid karta hai" is the general festive pattern, and the only number shown for it is Criteo's, labelled "pichhle saal".
- **It does not say costs are back to normal or better than October.** The three days after Diwali are back to about October's level. Three days can reverse.
- **"Customer badi sale mein busy tha" is our reading**, not a measured cause. It fits the published picture (Redseer via Inc42, 23 Oct 2026: about 100 million shoppers on the big online sales, 8–18 Oct; Counterpoint: "Platforms have seen strong traffic, but conversions have been weak").
- "Enquiry" is a message conversation started. It is not a booking or an order. No account that tracks purchases qualified for the comparison.
- The bidders (₹50, ₹120, ₹200), the shop, the daily budget stickers and the roti with ten plates are illustrations.
