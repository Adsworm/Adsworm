## Post text (LinkedIn, YouTube description)

Did your enquiries cost more this Diwali? Ours did.

We checked 18 of our ad accounts. The cost of one enquiry:
₹147 in September.
₹165 in October.
₹210 in Diwali week.

The easy answer is "festive season, so ads got expensive". In October, they did not. Showing an ad a thousand times cost about 5% less. So did a click. A smaller share of the people who clicked sent a message.

Before you blame your ads, check two numbers: the price of showing the ad, and the cost of an enquiry.

Notes on the data: 18 Meta ad accounts, read on 12 November 2026. An enquiry is a chat started from an ad, not a booking. The figure for 9–11 November is three days of data and can reverse. No client is named.
