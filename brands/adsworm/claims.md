# Adsworm: claims allowed on screen

**Sources:** adsworm.com and adsworm.in (read 2026-10-21), plus @adsworm captions.

**Rule:** a number may appear on screen only if it is in the "Use" column and the owner has confirmed it.

|---|---|
| 280+ brands scaled | adsworm.com, adsworm.in |
| 98% client retention | both |

| Perfect 5 Clinic: "We scaled from 7 to 11 branches because patient demand outgrew our clinic capacity." (Preeti Sabarwal & Swati Kundra, co-founders) | adsworm.com testimonial, named |

## Conflicting: fix the sites before using
- **Peak ROAS.** 12x (.com) vs 8.4x (.in).
- **Perfect 5 branches.** 11 pan-India (.com) vs 7 in Delhi NCR (.in) vs "1 → 15" (Instagram, June 2026).
- **₹50Cr+.** "ad spend managed" (.com) vs "revenue generated" (.in).
- **ROAS guarantee.** "5x ROAS guaranteed" (.in hero) vs "We don't promise results… That's not a guarantee" (.com).

## Never in paid ads
- Any ROAS guarantee ("5x guaranteed", "6–12x in writing"). Meta disapproves of unrealistic or guaranteed outcomes.
- Client names or testimonials, unless there is written permission for ad use.
